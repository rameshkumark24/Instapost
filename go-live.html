How the finished product works

A normal day

What you do

  • Tick cards on the review issue when a New cards to review message arrives — about five minutes, whenever fewer than ten are waiting.
  • Glance at the 19:45 confirmation.
  • Forward any message marked failed.
  • Top up the concept bank every seven months or so.

What it does on its own

  • Chooses the news story and skips a night rather than post filler.
  • Writes an original summary checked against the source, or quotes the publisher with credit.
  • Drafts tech-metaphor lines, and never posts one you haven't ticked.
  • Falls back to another AI model when one is slow or retired.
  • Warns you 14 days before the Instagram token expires, and tells you when anything fails.

News account

  • One post a day, only when a story clears the quality bar.
  • The card carries a headline and either an AI summary in its own words or the publisher's words in quotation marks, credited to them.
  • The caption links the source.

Tech-metaphor account

  • One post a day from the cards you approved, oldest first.
  • The caption explains what the technical term really means.
  • With nothing approved, it posts nothing and tells you so.

Controls. Hold one night by replying hold to the bot — or hold news, hold tech — and undo it with resume. If Telegram is down, commit state/hold.flag to the repo instead: the publisher checks for it at 19:45, and it holds both accounts every night until you delete it. Stop posting entirely by deleting the LIVE variable — builds keep running, nothing publishes. Stop everything by disabling the workflow in the Actions tab.

How it's being built

Prove the riskiest step first.

Gate A leads your list because if Meta won't accept a post, nothing else matters. It costs an evening to find out.

Nothing goes public by default.

48 builds have run and none could post. Publishing takes one deliberate switch, and removing it stops everything at once.

You approve the jokes; code checks the facts.

No tech-metaphor card posts without your tick. News summaries are checked against their source — today's "duopoly" claim traced back to the tweet itself.

Every failure speaks.

Skips, failures and review requests all reach your phone. A day with no message at all is itself the alarm.

Real runs over assumptions.

Live runs found what tests could not: a schedule drifting nearly ten hours, famous repositories crowding out news, a shut-down AI model, a token echoed back in an error message, a ten-minute drafting step. Each was reproduced, fixed, and covered by a test.
